### Question 1: Project Title
**SafeStep UG: A Privacy-First AI Safety Companion for Children**

---

### Question 2: Short Project Summary (100–150 words)
SafeStep UG is an empathetic, child-centred digital safety companion that helps children recognise cyberbullying, coercive threats, and unwanted sexual requests online, empowering them to take immediate, safe action. Rather than secretly copying conversations or turning smartphones into surveillance wiretaps, SafeStep evaluates messages locally on the device with zero cloud retention by default. 

When a risk is detected, SafeStep provides calm, non-shaming guidance and child-controlled actions: pausing to calm down, muting or blocking the contact, saving tamper-evident local evidence, or reaching out to a designated trusted adult or Uganda’s toll-free Sauti 116 Child Helpline. If escalating to an adult, the child explicitly previews and confirms the single message snippet being shared. SafeStep balances timely safeguarding with children's fundamental privacy and dignity rights under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019.

### Question 4: What specific problem are you addressing?
Children using messaging apps and social platforms face hurtful insults, cyber harassment, financial blackmail (e.g. mobile money extortion), and coercive sexual solicitation. Existing responses generally suffer from two extremes:
1. **Total Inaction:** Leaving vulnerable children alone to interpret and cope with distressing, manipulative messages.
2. **Invasive Surveillance Spyware:** Commercial monitoring tools that silently upload all private chats, photos, and browsing logs to remote databases. This destroys child-parent trust, creates panic over false alarms, and violates children's legal privacy rights.

Children need a supportive tool that identifies risks early, explains them without shame, and puts safety choices firmly in their hands while providing a clear escalation path for serious dangers.

### Question 5: What will you build during the hackathon?
During the two-day hackathon, we will build and demonstrate a lightweight, high-fidelity web prototype consisting of:
1. **Simulated Child Chat Interface:** Demonstrating realistic, synthetic test scenarios (healthy debate, cyberbullying, financial coercion, sexual solicitation, and shielded image media) using invented, non-graphic data.
2. **On-Device Risk Classifier & Explainability Console:** Demonstrating real-time text analysis with transparent confidence scores and uncertainty margins (communicated as advisory signals, not proof of guilt).
3. **Child-Centred Action Hub:** Providing non-shaming guidance and immediate user actions: *Pause & Breathe*, *Mute Contact*, *Encrypted Local Evidence Vault*, and *False Alarm Correction*.
4. **Transparent Trusted-Adult Escalation Flow:** An interactive process allowing the child to select a trusted recipient (guardian, school counselor, or Sauti 116) and preview the exact snippet before confirming dispatch.
5. **Harmful Image Shield Mock:** Showing local blur and safe exit options without processing or storing explicit imagery.
6. **Luganda Benchmark Evaluation Lab:** Demonstrating explainable dialect evaluation while explicitly stating that native-speaker review is required before real-world deployment.

### Question 6: How will you protect children’s privacy?
Privacy by design is the foundation of SafeStep UG, directly aligned with Section 3 of Uganda’s *Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019*:
* **Data Minimisation:** No real children's data or sensitive datasets are collected or stored. Zero unsolicited messages, contacts, or GPS locations are transmitted to any cloud server.
* **On-Device Local Processing:** Risk inference occurs client-side in the device sandbox; raw conversations never leave the phone.
* **Transparent Consent:** SafeStep never secretly forwards chats. Adult escalation is strictly child-initiated with a full preview of the single message snippet.
* **Tamper-Evident Local Storage:** Evidence records are kept on the device with local SHA-256 integrity hashes and one-tap shredding/deletion.
* **Algorithmic Humility:** Uncertainty margins are clearly displayed, and false alarm dismissals immediately calibrate model sensitivity.
* **Institutional Governance:** No field deployment will occur without formal child-assent frameworks, parental consent, and ethical clearance from Ugandan child protection partners.
